A game made by Godot Engine.

### Gameplay
- You 'Andro' see a girl and you like her, then you go on an adventure for her and fight giant cats.
- You have workers, worker goes on a trip, brings food and put it in a chest. You then collect it and sell it.
- You gather money and upgrade your workers, health or buy weapons.
- Your goal is to reach 10000$.
- If you die, you lose your money, inventory, unlocked workers. You keep your added health and workers upgrades (You just have to rebuy the unlock.).

### Controls
#### PC
- Moving: WASD or arrows
- Sprint: shift
- Open inventory: Q
- Interact (Open chests): E
- Hit: Left click
- Next chat: left click or E
- Previous chat: right click
#### Phone
- You have buttons for each actions except for previousa chat
